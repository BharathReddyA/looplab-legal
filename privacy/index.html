Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 29, 2026

LoopLab ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use the LoopLab mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information
When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, and a hashed (encrypted) password.

Platform Connections
If you connect Spotify or YouTube Music, we receive an access token to read and create playlists on your behalf. We do not store your Spotify or Google passwords.

Usage Data
We collect playlists you generate, songs you create, and preferences you set within the app. This is stored securely in our database.

Audio Content
AI-generated songs are uploaded to Cloudinary (a secure cloud storage service) and linked to your account.

Voice Input
If you use the voice input feature, audio is temporarily processed by Google Gemini for transcription and is not stored by us.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve the LoopLab service
  • To generate AI playlists and songs personalized to your preferences
  • To sync playlists to your connected Spotify or YouTube Music account
  • To display your saved songs and playlists in your profile
  • To enable the Discover feature where you can share playlists publicly

3. Third-Party Services

LoopLab uses the following third-party services:

  • Spotify API — to read and create playlists
  • YouTube Data API — to read and create playlists
  • Google Gemini AI — to generate playlist recommendations and transcribe voice input
  • ElevenLabs — to generate AI music
  • Cloudinary — to store generated audio files
  • MongoDB Atlas — to store user data securely
  • Railway — to host our backend services

Each of these services has their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review them.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We only share data with the third-party services listed above as necessary to provide the LoopLab service.

If you make a playlist public in the Discover section, other LoopLab users will be able to see it. You can change visibility to private at any time from your Profile tab.

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including encrypted passwords (bcrypt), JWT authentication tokens, and HTTPS for all data transmission. Access tokens for Spotify and Google are stored securely on your device using encrypted storage.

6. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us at the email below.

7. Children's Privacy

LoopLab is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected such information, please contact us immediately.

8. Your Rights

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Disconnect Spotify or YouTube Music at any time from your Profile
  • Make your playlists private or delete them at any time

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
